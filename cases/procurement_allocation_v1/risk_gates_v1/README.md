# Procurement allocation risk gates v1

This held-out adaptive panel diagnoses two residual failure mechanisms from the
qualified V4 procurement strategy without reusing any development or confirmatory
world.

| Stratum | Worlds | Decision pressure |
|---|---:|---|
| `sample_timing` | 3 | Verbal sample-turnaround confirmation and serial critical-path scheduling |
| `landed_cash` | 3 | Quote-level freight, duty, MOQ, BOM multiplicity, and cash-budget arithmetic |

Every world appears in `labeled/` and `opaque/`. The mirrors preserve objectives,
supplier terms, substantive listing claims, world seeds, and objective upper bounds.
Only supplier identifiers, neutral display names, and listing order differ.

`sample_logistics` inquiries return `sample_lead_time_days` and `sample_cost_usd` as
verbal claims. They do not create formal offers or verified samples. Awards still
require an unexpired environment-issued offer, an exact-variant verified sample,
and all objective constraints.

All supplier economics are synthetic experimental variables. Component-family
selection is grounded in the frozen 231-project snapshot; these cases do not claim
current supplier pricing, availability, capacity, or commercial authority.

The paired campaign has four prompt conditions: frozen V4, temporal gate only, cash
gate only, and joint gates. With two surfaces, six worlds, and three inference seeds,
the plan contains 144 scored trajectories. Results are adaptive mechanism evidence,
not a population model ranking or confirmatory claim.
