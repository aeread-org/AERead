# Controlled campaign entry

`aeread campaign --contract configs/housing_budget_campaign_v1.json --output-root runs/housing-budget-inspection` creates a design inspection artifact without constructing a live provider client. The second allowlisted contract is `configs/datacenter_terms_budget_campaign_v1.json`. Exchange `aeread run` retains its existing behavior.

Choose `--stage provider_free` or `--stage profile_admission` to stop after the corresponding cumulative gate. `--stage live` explicitly enables provider execution. Every invocation requires a fresh empty root and atomically claims it before gates or client creation. A design root cannot be continued or resumed. Existing results and summaries are never used as cache hits.

Live executes design validation, the family's real provider-free gate, and static profile admission in its own root before creating its provider client. The library `aeread.campaign_cli.run_campaign` accepts an injected provider factory for offline testing. Optional injected account preflight reports `passed_snapshot`, `failed`, `unsupported` or `unknown`. Required preflight fails closed unless a snapshot passes. The built-in default is unsupported and verifies no actual account, key, balance or shared external consumption. No account API call is invented.

## Identity and resources

The two new envelopes pin the base contract's canonical digest and exact local source digests. Existing strict family validators read the unchanged base contracts. Envelope resource declarations must equal prepared profiles: dollar cap, base output tokens, effective length retry ceiling, exact profile digests, declared attempts and retryable conditions. A profile ID with seed-varying sampling can have several exact digests. The launcher derives the complete ordered cell/arm matrix independently from the strict base contract, checks prepared metadata and model/seed assignments, and requires each bound cell and its case hash to belong to its verified plan. The actual plan case, cell case hash and strict base case must agree; the DC setup case facade must also agree. Housing derives the exact unchanged v1 case manifest from its base configuration, including header and provenance, without building another setup. Every planned cell records its actual seat-to-profile assignment and run-plan identity. Multiple seats assigned to one profile share that profile's cap within the cell's executor. They do not have independent per-seat caps.

Housing preserves the serial model-sensitivity case panel and rotated condition order, .01 profile caps, existing attempt eligibility and effective 8x token ceiling. Datacenter terms prepares each plan once, with its declared case slug and pricing, and executes that same setup. Existing Housing and datacenter campaign loops are unchanged. The envelopes do not resolve Housing D-4 resource comparability, alter scientific inclusion, establish model rankings or adopt policies from other open branches. Installed third-party dependencies and provider behavior are outside these local source digests.

## Admission and halts

Controls apply to one invocation. Before dispatch, known recorded spend plus active cell reserves plus the next reserve must fit the campaign cost limit. Reserves are admission estimates, never charges or a hard provider-dollar limit. A cell may spend beyond its reserve. Settlement retains all known failed-call charges. Unknown outcomes retain a lower bound even when a numeric fee is known. A lower bound below the limit does not prove the total stayed within the limit; the summary reports that comparison as null.

The controller persists each cell before releasing its reserve, observing its outcome and admitting replacements. Account faults stop queued work immediately when observed. Other operational failures use the declared streak in completion-observation order; success resets the streak. Already available completions are observed before new dispatch. A family-critical Housing failure also stops admission. Started work drains after a halt so that delayed fees and evidence remain visible. Cancellation, finalization errors and persistence errors stop new dispatch, preserve the available evidence prefix, attempt to mark the invocation incomplete and propagate. No completed summary is written for such an invocation. A disk failure can prevent even the incomplete marker from being written.

## Output and accounting

The root contains the sealed contract, design inventory, gates, preflight report, sealed per-cell rows and a sealed summary. Artifacts are fsynced as temporary files, published exclusively without overwrite, and their parent directory is fsynced. Return-value normalization precedes I/O. Temporary cleanup and every post-link persistence step remain inside the publication transaction; failure rolls back the published artifact. Error-path cleanup is best effort and preserves the primary exception. All rows and the summary join the launch contract, controls, source pins, plan and cell identities. Before publishing a row, the launcher loads its sealed receipt from the exact expected RunLayout attempt path, checks its digest, plan ID, plan hash, cell ID, evidence seal and canonical spending, and compares callback status, inclusion and scores to that receipt. Conflicting callback identities and duplicate receipt provenance are rejected. Completed scores come from actual verified family receipts and matching offline replay. Failed cells retain their actual receipt, original provider cause and final receipt condition separately. Not-attempted cells have no receipt, score or fictional charge.

Overall and per arm, `planned = completed + attempted_failure + not_attempted` and `attempted = completed + attempted_failure`. Every planned arm remains present even with zero attempts. Canonical spend totals include completed and failed cells, qualifier and unsettled-call counts; reserves do not enter those totals. `exact` means complete accounting of recorded costs, not reconciliation to a provider invoice.

For timeout and transport, legacy terminal `cost_usd` remains `"unknown"`. Supported numeric fees appear as additive `known_cost_usd` with `cost_accounting="lower_bound"`; the canonical spend reader counts them once while retaining unsettled status. Explicit valid provider dollars do not require complete token fields. Token-price fallback requires complete valid input/output counts and valid cached counts no larger than input; absent or malformed usage is not zero-cost evidence. No partial token price estimate changes admission or retry eligibility.

The accounting increment covers supported usage and known charges delivered to the executor. It does not add usage extraction to every HTTP or SDK error producer. In particular, existing non-stream OpenRouter SDK exceptions can classify an error without attaching error-body usage, and `with_reported_usage` retains its existing completion-choice admission requirement. Producer changes owned by #250 and #248, and a combined #248 producer plus controlled-campaign tree, are outside this artifact and remain unverified. No claim of complete coverage of all #226 billing paths is made.
