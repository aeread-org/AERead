with no supply, losing $617, which is −288% of that world's gap. In the other 19 twins it closes 78% on average. The
declared figure is the mean over all 20, and it is not trimmed here.

**Reading.** With odds that make testing plainly worth it, Sol tests everywhere and usually does better than the
one-step rule, while Opus still never tests. Paired by world, Sol's share on a twin minus its share on the original
world is +24 points on average, with an SD of 103 points that the walk-away dominates. Nothing here is a hypothesis
test.
